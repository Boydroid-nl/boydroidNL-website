Together with Cipher and commissioned by Funeplan Group B.V., Boydroid developed the Memorial App, an innovative app for keeping and sharing memories of loved ones digitally. The app bridges the physical resting place and digital memories, so stories and moments are preserved for future generations.

## A digital history book for cemeteries

The Memorial App turns cemeteries into interactive places where stories come to life:

- **Personal memorial profiles:** create a digital memorial with photos, texts and stories
- **Direct linking:** connects the profile to a physical location such as a grave or an urn in the Memorial Cube
- **Flexible privacy options:** decide for yourself who can access the memorial page
- **Intuitive navigation:** easily find the right location on the cemetery map

## Unique integration with the Memorial Cube

The app offers dedicated features for Memorial Cube users:

- **Access control:** manage placing and removing the urn yourself
- **Reservation system:** easily schedule a time for interment
- **Digital linking:** connect the physical urn to a digital memorial profile

## A dignified place for memories

The Memorial App is designed with respect for:

- The privacy of the bereaved
- The dignity of the deceased
- The need for a lasting connection with loved ones
- Sharing memories with future generations

This makes the Memorial App an essential link in the modern grieving process, bringing physical and digital memories together in one respectful place.
