PorteRenee.nl is the largest independent personal finance platform in the Netherlands, run by financefluencer Renée Lamboo and her husband Kasper Kooij. Every month they reach hundreds of thousands of people with tips, tricks and inspiration on how to be smart with money. They do that through their online platform, through the popular PorteRenee podcast, and through the free PorteRenee app for Android and iPhone, made by Boydroid.
