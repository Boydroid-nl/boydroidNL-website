## A complete public transport app for operators

In close collaboration with [Infoplaza](https://www.infoplaza.com/nl/mobiliteit), Boydroid built an advanced white-label public transport app that makes travelling by public transport easy. This complete mobility solution combines real-time travel information with ease of use and smart functionality.

## An all-in-one travel solution

The app offers travellers a complete set of features:

- **Digital travel:** easily buy and activate e-tickets right in the app, check in and out with your smartphone, and keep full insight into your travel history
- **Real-time travel information:** see current departure times and live vehicle locations, and get instant alerts about disruptions or diversions on your favourite routes
- **Smart route planning:** plan your trip door to door with an advanced planner that integrates every form of public transport and shared mobility
- **A personal travel experience:** save your favourite stops and lines, receive personalised push notifications and get local weather forecasts

## Reliable technology

The app is built on Infoplaza's robust data platform, which provides:

- Accurate and reliable real-time information
- Seamless integration with different operators
- Optimal performance and usability
- Continuous development of new features

## A white-label solution

The app can be fully adapted to the branding and specific needs of regional transit companies, giving travellers a familiar and trusted experience. It is already live with leading operators such as RET, HTM and Breng.
