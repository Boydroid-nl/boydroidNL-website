## Your court, your time slot

With Padook you can quickly and easily book a padel or tennis court at your club, all in one app. Whether you play every week or spontaneously, Padook gives you the overview and the control.

## What you can do with Padook

- Choose your location and see available times
- Book your court in a few simple steps
- Pay securely with iDEAL or other payment options via Mollie
- View and manage your bookings in your personal overview
- Cancel for free up to 24 hours in advance

## Who is Padook for?

For every player who values overview and convenience. Whether you are just starting out or play every week, Padook gets you on court in no time. Padook is used by sports clubs in the Netherlands and is growing fast.
