## Smart guidance for digital legacy

Commissioned by and in collaboration with Cipher and Funeplan Group B.V., Boydroid built the Funeplan App: a groundbreaking solution that combines artificial intelligence with years of expertise in digital legacy and funeral planning.

## What makes Funeplan unique

The app stands out through:

- **Advanced AI integration:** with Fune, our AI assistant, we built the first digital legacy expert that is available 24/7
- **Extensive knowledge base:** the app draws on years of expertise from Digital Life Legacy as well as current laws and regulations
- **Complete digital support:** from pre-arrangement to ceremony planning, all in one easy-to-use app

## Core services

- Digital legacy expert
- Direct advice on digital assets and accounts
- Support with legal and practical questions
- Smart funeral planning
- Digital coordination with funeral directors
- Electronic signing and document management
- Uploading and managing ceremony materials

## Flexible implementation

For Funeplan we developed a white-label platform that adapts to the different branding and workflows of funeral companies. By combining our technical expertise with Digital Life Legacy's industry knowledge, we created a solution that strengthens the service to end users.
